v1.0.1 (Fleet Transparency & Control)

Advanced Fleet Oversight & Granular Data Control

1) The Actual Updates

We have significantly enhanced the fleet management module to provide deeper operational insights and smoother data management:

  • Enhanced Driver Registry: The drivers table now includes dedicated columns for License Type and Past Accidents, allowing for immediate safety assessment at a glance.
  • Refined Data Display: Removed redundant fields like vehicle passwords to keep the interface focused. Passwords for drivers remain visible via a secure Show/Hide (👁️) toggle.
  • Intelligent Input Controls: Upgraded the Edit Modules for both drivers and vehicles. License and Vehicle types are now selectable via Dropdown Menus (Bike, Scooty, Van, Truck, etc.) to prevent manual entry errors and ensure data consistency.
  • Consolidated Communication: Simplified driver registration by merging duplicate phone sections into a single, standardized "Contact Number" field.
  • Comprehensive CRUD Oversight: Managers now have full authority to edit every system field for fleet assets, including credentials, hub assignments, and operational metrics.

2) Impact & User Benefits

These refinements translate to measurable improvements for our core user base:

  • For Fleet Managers: Faster decision-making with enriched data tables. The addition of accident history and license types directly on the dashboard enables quicker driver dispatching and risk assessment.
  • For Data Administrators: Drastic reduction in data entry errors. Dropdown selections for vehicle types ensure that fleet categorization remains uniform across the entire platform.
  • For Support Teams: Improved troubleshooting efficiency. Direct access to driver credentials with secure toggling means login issues can be resolved in seconds without deep database queries.

v1.0.0 (Initial Launch)

Complete Platform Overview & Features

Welcome to the first version of Logistix. After extensive development, we are proud to present a fully digital, cashless, and AI-optimized logistics infrastructure. Here are the core features currently live:

  • AI-Powered Warehouse Management: Strategic hub deployment with intelligent suggestion algorithms, water-body detection, and terrain verification to ensure optimal infrastructure placement.
  • Real-Time Fleet Intelligence: A high-performance Digital Twin environment featuring live tracking with traffic simulation, driver fatigue monitoring, and instant breakdown alerts.
  • Advanced Shipment Lifecycle: End-to-end management with bulk upload capabilities, multi-leg shipment splitting (Manual & Auto), and OTP-based secure delivery verification.
  • Fintech Engine (Paisa-Fast): A digital escrow and payment system allowing drivers to request emergency funds (Fuel, Food, Tolls) with automated manager approval workflows and real-time ledger tracking.
  • Predictive Analytics: Cascading impact predictor that simulates downstream delays and recommends proactive rerouting strategies based on active transit deviations.
  • Safety & Wellness Center: 24/7 monitoring of driver vitals via wearable integration, stress index tracking, and automated "Zen Mode" triggers for high-fatigue patterns.
  • Simulation Sandbox: Test disaster scenarios (cyclones, floods, heatwaves, riots) on a map to see real-time impact on your supply chain and receive AI mitigation strategies.
  • Multi-Language Support: Full platform localization in English, Hindi, and Bengali to empower a diverse workforce with native language interfaces.
  • Strategy Oracle: Business growth forecasting tool to simulate revenue, profit, and ROI based on expansion parameters like warehouse automation and green fleet ratios.
  • Voice Command Interface: Hands-free control for drivers, allowing them to report incidents, check tasks, and navigate using simple voice instructions.
  • Blockchain-Verified Ledger: Secure transaction history for all payments and reward points, ensuring transparency and trust across the network.
  • Dynamic Routing & Traffic: Real-time route optimization using OSRM with simulated traffic overlays to provide the most efficient paths.
